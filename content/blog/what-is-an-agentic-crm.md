## What is an agentic CRM?

An agentic CRM is a customer relationship management system that uses AI agents to understand customer context, identify what needs attention, and propose or execute the next step within defined permissions. Unlike a traditional CRM that primarily stores information and tracks activity, an agentic CRM actively helps sales teams move deals forward.

For Indian B2B sales teams, this means less time updating records, chasing internal reminders, and searching through conversations. It means having a system that understands the relationship behind each deal and helps representatives act on it.

## How is an agentic CRM different from a traditional CRM?

A traditional CRM is a system of record. It stores leads, contacts, companies, opportunities, activities, and notes. Your sales team is responsible for interpreting that information and deciding what to do next.

An **AI CRM** adds intelligence to this process. However, there is an important distinction between adding an AI chatbot to an existing CRM and building a CRM around agents that can understand context and participate in workflows.

Consider a sales representative managing 40 active opportunities. A traditional CRM can show the representative which deals are open. An agentic CRM can identify which deals need attention, explain why, and prepare the next action.

The difference is not simply automation. It is context-aware action.

## What does an agentic CRM do that a classic CRM doesn't?

An agentic CRM introduces a layer of intelligence between customer information and sales execution. Here are seven practical differences:

- **Reads customer context.** Understands relevant information across available conversations, documents, emails and deal records instead of treating every activity as an isolated entry.
- **Identifies what needs attention.** Notices unanswered enquiries, pending commitments, changing customer intent, and opportunities that have gone quiet.
- **Proposes next steps.** Recommends follow-ups, tasks, replies and other actions based on the customer's actual situation.
- **Prepares customer communication.** Drafts replies using approved campaign information, FAQs, PDFs and available business context.
- **Interprets customer intent.** Helps classify replies as Hot, Warm or Cold so representatives can prioritise their attention.
- **Builds deal intelligence.** Creates a concise brief for each deal, giving representatives a clearer understanding of its history and current status.
- **Executes within permissions.** Can carry out permitted actions while requesting human approval for changes that require review.

These capabilities turn a CRM from a passive database into an active participant in the sales workflow.

## How does an agentic CRM work in a real sales team?

The easiest way to understand a **CRM with AI agents** is to look at everyday situations.

### Example 1: An open pricing question after a customer call

Imagine a sales representative at Kaveri Textiles has a call with a prospective distributor. The distributor is interested in placing an order but wants clarification on bulk pricing. The representative promises to share the revised pricing structure the following morning.

In a conventional CRM, the representative must remember to write a note, create a task and follow up.

In an agentic CRM, the workflow can be more connected. The **Call Agent** processes a supported call recording and generates a transcript, summary, customer intent and relevant tasks. The pricing question becomes visible as an unresolved item associated with the deal. The representative can review the generated information and confirm the next action. **Copilot** can propose a change or task, but asks for confirmation before making changes.

The next morning, the representative has the relevant context ready instead of having to reconstruct the conversation.

The important distinction is that the system recognises an outstanding commitment, not just a completed call.

### Example 2: A customer who suddenly stops ordering

Consider Sundaram Mills, a customer that usually places an order every month. For the past three months, the customer has ordered consistently. This month, however, there has been no new order or meaningful engagement.

A traditional CRM may continue to show Sundaram Mills as an active account. An agentic CRM can help identify the unusual inactivity by considering available account history and recorded interactions. It can flag the account for attention and propose a check-in. The account manager can then review the context and decide whether to contact the customer.

This is particularly valuable for Indian distributors, manufacturers and suppliers where repeat business and long-term relationships contribute significantly to revenue. The agent doesn't need to make assumptions about why the customer stopped ordering. It simply helps the team notice a change that might otherwise go unnoticed.

## What AI agents should actually do inside a CRM

The term AI agent gets used broadly, so it helps to distinguish between specific capabilities. At HeiGin, we approach agentic CRM through specialised agents with defined responsibilities:

- **Copilot** acts as an assistant for CRM operations. It understands requests, prepares proposed changes, and asks for confirmation before every change.
- **Sales Agent** drafts email replies using the relevant campaign's own offer, FAQs, PDFs and available information. It fact-checks its drafts against those sources instead of inventing commercial claims.
- **Intent scoring** classifies customer replies as Hot, Warm or Cold, helping sales teams prioritise conversations based on expressed interest.
- **Deal brief** creates one concise brief per deal, bringing together relevant information so representatives can understand the opportunity without searching through every interaction.
- **Hinglish support** understands the way many Indian customers actually communicate, including conversations that mix Hindi and English.
- **Documents Q&A** answers questions about business documents and provides citations so users can trace answers back to their sources.
- **Call agent** processes supported Hinglish calls into transcripts, summaries, intent signals and actionable tasks.

Together, these agents address different parts of the sales process rather than forcing every problem through one general-purpose chatbot.

## Can AI agents operate without human supervision?

They can operate within defined boundaries, but unrestricted autonomy is not the goal. Sales activities have different levels of commercial risk. Generating a deal summary is relatively low risk. Sending an email to a prospective customer or changing a quotation can have direct business consequences.

An effective agentic CRM should distinguish between these actions. HeiGin uses six permission roles — Admin, Sales Manager, Senior AE, AE, SDR and Read-only — that determine what users can access and which operations they can perform. Outbound communication and quotation changes default to review. The system also maintains an admin-only audit log of agent proposals and actions.

This gives businesses a way to introduce AI into everyday operations without giving every agent unrestricted access to customer records or commercial decisions.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between an AI CRM and an agentic CRM?

An AI CRM uses artificial intelligence to assist with activities such as writing, summarisation and analysis. An agentic CRM uses specialised AI agents to understand context, identify required actions, and propose or execute tasks within defined permissions.

### Can an agentic CRM send emails automatically?

It depends on its configuration and permissions. In HeiGin, the Sales Agent drafts replies using campaign-specific information. Outbound communication defaults to review.

### Is an agentic CRM suitable for small businesses?

Yes. SMB sales teams can benefit from automated follow-ups, customer context, lead prioritisation and reduced administrative work without needing a large sales operations team.

### Does an agentic CRM replace sales representatives?

No. It helps representatives manage information, identify opportunities and prepare actions. Relationship building, negotiation and important commercial decisions remain human responsibilities.

### What integrations should an agentic CRM support?

Useful integrations depend on the sales process. Indian B2B businesses may prioritise Gmail, WhatsApp, IndiaMART, JustDial, Facebook Lead Ads, telephony, spreadsheets and business productivity tools.
